# CoverSpell steering — the "tease, don't spoil" rule
Every headline this app produces must follow one rule:
1. State only the premise (what the audience may know).
2. End with an unanswered question.
3. NEVER include: the twist, the outcome, result numbers, or conclusion words (can't, won't, actually, in the end, 反而, 涨不了, 已经…).
If a draft violates rule 3, the spoiler checker must flag the exact words.
Keep the UI playful and haunted (Costume Contest) but never at the cost of clarity.
